Overview

External attack surface management, kept small

EASM answers one question: which of our systems can someone on the internet reach right now, and what do they see when they look? This tool answers it for the assets you already know about, on a schedule, inside the SIEM your analysts already use.

Who it is for

SOC and blue teams that want new internet-facing services and exposure changes to appear as Splunk events, next to the rest of their detections.

What it produces

Normalized exposure events with a risk score and a change flag, ready for dashboards and alerts.

Pipeline

One run, six steps

Every run follows the same path from your asset list to a Splunk dashboard. Select a step to see what happens in it.

    Data sources

    Five passive sources, one always on

    Your targets go in, and each configured source is asked about every IP. A source without credentials is skipped, so a run with no API keys still works using the free source.

    CTEM

    Where the pipeline fits in Continuous Threat Exposure Management

    CTEM is a five-stage cycle that Gartner defines for reducing exposure over time. The pipeline covers the early stages directly and feeds the later ones through Splunk. Select a stage on the cycle.

    Extend

    Built to grow

    Data sources, risk rules and delivery targets are separate parts of the tool, so adding one does not mean changing the others.

    New data source

    Each source is its own small client. Add one more and it joins every run once it is configured.

    Your own risk rules

    Severity comes from an editable rule set. Change what counts as critical for your environment without touching code.

    More destinations

    Splunk is connected today. The same delivery step can feed other tools.
